About

Marc LeMay is a composer known for music that is at once “pleasingly muscular” and “passionately lyrical” (New York Classical Review). He has written for singer Samantha Rose Williams and the American Patriots Project, the BrassTaps Duo, the CHAI Collaborative Ensemble, and Variant Six, among many others. Much of Marc’s work is collaborative in nature. In addition to his work with librettists and poets, he co-created a number of multimedia pieces, including dance dramas, music for theatre and film, an app-based interactive soundwalk, and orchestral arrangements for singer-songwriters. He holds a PhD in composition from the University of Pennsylvania and an MA in composition from the University of Michigan, and most recently held teaching positions at Florida State University and Georgia State University.
